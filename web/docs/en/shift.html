Opening and closing your shift

A shift ties every order and every pound you handle to you and to one branch drawer. You open it before your first order and close it after your last.

ForStaffLine manager

✕No shift, no POS

The app sends you to Start Shift and keeps you there until there is an open shift on the branch you selected that you opened yourself. A colleague's open shift does not count, and neither does your own shift on a different branch.

Opening your shift

  1. 1

    Pick the branch first

    The Select POS Profile screen (or the branch name in the header) decides which drawer the shift belongs to. Picking the wrong branch here is the single most common cause of a shift that will not close.

  2. 2

    Count the cash in the drawer

    The screen says "Count opening cash and enter it" and shows the branch, the cash account and the System Balance - what the system thinks is in there. Count the real money anyway.

  3. 3

    Enter what you counted

    Type the counted amount into Counted Opening Cash. The app shows the Difference against the system balance as you type. Enter the real number even when it does not match - that is the whole point of counting.

  4. 4

    Tap Start Shift

    You land on the order screen and the header shows Shift Active with the time you started.

⚠Cash only

Count the physical cash for this branch and nothing else. InstaPay, wallet and card money is tracked by the system on its own - you never type those in at shift start.

Closing your shift

  1. 1

    Clear the board first

    Every order for the day should be in its right column, and every payment recorded. An order left in the middle of the board is a question somebody has to answer tomorrow.

  2. 2

    Settle your couriers

    If a courier still owes the branch (or the branch owes them), the app blocks the close - see below.

  3. 3

    Open End Shift

    Menu → End Shift. The entry only appears when the open shift on the selected branch is yours.

  4. 4

    Count the drawer and enter it

    The screen says "Count the cash in the drawer and enter the amount" and shows nothing else - no expected total, no difference. That is deliberate: you count what is actually there, then the system compares.

  5. 5

    Tap End Shift

    Now the summary appears: expected, counted, and the difference per payment method, plus the invoice count and the day's totals. If there is a difference, the system posts a cash over/short entry by itself - you do not adjust anything.

  6. 6

    You are signed out

    Closing the shift signs you out on purpose, so the next person on the device starts on their own account.

"Settle courier balances before ending the shift"

This is the most common thing that stops a close. The message names how many courier transactions are still open, across how many couriers and how many orders, and it lists each courier with their net balance.

Tap Review & Settle Couriers, settle what is pending, then come back and close. Settling couriers is part of the staff job - you do not need a manager for it.

ℹFull details

How to read the balances and settle them is on the Courier money page.

When it will not open or close

▸"Shift Already Open" - started by someone else
The branch already has an open shift started by another user, and it must be closed before a new one starts. If that person has gone home, a line manager can close it from Shift Monitor.
▸"You have an open shift on profile X"
You left a shift open on another branch. Use Switch to active shift profile then Go to End Shift, close it, and come back. You can only hold one open shift at a time.
▸"Cash entry is unavailable"
The shift has no closing payment method configured, so there is nothing to count against. Reopen the shift or contact support - this is a setup problem, not something you can fix at the branch.
▸I counted wrong and already closed
Tell your line manager the same day. The difference is already posted as a cash over/short entry; correcting it is an accounting job, not a re-count.
