Start here

Everything a branch staff member or a line manager needs to run a day on Jarz POS - sign in, open a shift, take orders, move them on the board, settle the courier, close out.

ForStaffLine manager

ℹThis guide covers two roles only

Staff (Jarz POS Staff) and line manager (JARZ line manager). Owner, accounting, B2B sales, production and purchasing screens are deliberately left out - if a menu entry is not described here, it is not part of your job.

The day, end to end

Sign in→Pick your branch→Start Shift→Take orders→Move them on the board→Settle couriers→End Shift

The app will not let you skip a step: no order screen without an open shift, and no shift close while a courier still owes money.

Which one am I?

Open the menu and look at what is in it. Your role decides which entries exist, and the guide marks every action with the role that can do it.

Two things worth knowing before you start

  • •The app speaks Arabic. Menu → Language switches the whole app between English and Arabic. This guide has the same page in both, and the button in the header of every page switches it.
  • •Everything is per branch. The branch you pick at the top of POS or the board (the POS profile) decides which orders you see, which stock you sell from, and which cash drawer your shift belongs to.
▸A menu entry is missing compared to my colleague
The menu is built from your roles. If you need an entry you do not have, ask your manager to change your role - it is not a setting on the device.
▸I tapped something and got "Not permitted"
A few entries appear for line managers that the server still refuses (Cash Transfer, Purchase Invoice, Stock Transfer, Inventory Count, and most of Reports). That is expected - they belong to the manager tier. Nothing broke.
▸Which version am I on?
Menu → About shows the build, the environment and the patch status. Have that screen open when you report a problem.
