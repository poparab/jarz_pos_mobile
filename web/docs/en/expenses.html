Expenses and item requests

Two different things on purpose: money that already left the drawer, and stock you are running out of.

ForStaffLine manager

Recording an expense

Menu → Expenses → New Expense. Record it the same day - an expense remembered next week is an expense nobody can verify.

FieldWhat to put in it
ReasonThe expense account - the category the money went to. Pick the closest one; do not force it into whatever is at the top.
Pay fromWhere the money actually came from - the branch drawer, or another payment source. Getting this wrong makes your shift not balance.
AmountWhat was actually spent.
Expense dateThe day the money left, not the day you are typing.
RemarksOptional, but this is where you say who, what and why. Write it.

★Approval is manager-tier

Staff and line managers both see Submit for approval, and both get "Expense submitted for approval". Only a JARZ Manager can approve - a line manager cannot approve their own or anyone else's.

  • •The month view totals Approved and Pending separately, so you can see what is still waiting.
  • •Timeline on an expense shows who did what to it and when.

Asking for stock

Menu → Item Requests. Anyone can raise one - deliberately, because the person who notices the shortage is usually not the person who buys.

  1. 1

    Tap +

    Request items - add the items you need with quantities.

  2. 2

    Say when you need it

    Needed by is what turns a request into a priority. A request with a real date gets bought; one without drifts.

  3. 3

    Add a note

    Brand, size, urgency - whatever the person buying would otherwise have to call and ask you.

  4. 4

    Send it

    Track it under Mine. It moves through Waiting → Ordered → Partly bought → Bought, and can come back Rejected with a reason.

ℹNot the same thing

An expense is money that already left. An item request is a thing you still need. Buying against a request is a manager job; asking is everyone's.
