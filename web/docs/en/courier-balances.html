Courier balances and settling

Who owes whom, and how to clear it. You settle couriers yourself - it is not a manager job, and your shift will not close until it is done.

ForStaffLine manager

Opening it

Menu → Courier Balances, or from the board header menu. It opens as a list of couriers with a Net figure each.

Reading a balance

LabelMeaningWhat you do
Collect From CourierThe courier is holding the branch's money - they collected more from customers than their delivery fees come to.Take the cash and settle.
Pay CourierThe branch owes the courier - their delivery fees come to more than they collected.Pay them from the drawer and settle.
SettledNothing outstanding.Nothing.

ℹThe net is order minus shipping

The settlement screen writes it out: Collect (Order - Shipping). The courier keeps their delivery fee and hands over the rest; when the fee is bigger than what they collected, the direction flips and the branch pays them.

Settling

  1. 1

    Open the courier

    Tapping a courier shows Details - every order behind that balance, with its city and amount. Check the list against what the courier is telling you.

  2. 2

    Preview before you settle

    The preview button shows exactly what the settlement will post. Use it when the number surprises you.

  3. 3

    Count the money first

    Hand over or take the cash, and only then confirm. Settling in the app is the record of a thing that already happened.

  4. 4

    Settle, or Settle All

    Settle clears one order. Settle All clears every open order for that courier at once and asks you to confirm the total first - use it at end of shift when the courier is standing there.

⚠Do not settle twice

If the confirmation seems slow, wait - do not tap again. Two taps have double-paid orders before. If you are unsure whether it went through, reopen the balances and look.

Questions

▸"No couriers found"
Nobody has an open balance on this branch - which is what you want at the end of a shift. If you expected somebody, check you are on the right branch.
▸"Nothing to pay or collect"
The order's amount and the delivery fee cancel out. Settle it anyway to clear it off the shift.
▸The courier disagrees with the total
Open Details and go order by order. Nine times out of ten it is one order that was marked Pay Now when the courier actually collected, or the other way round - your line manager can correct the collection method.
