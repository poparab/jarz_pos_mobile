Sign in

Opening the app, signing in, choosing your language, turning on order alerts, and signing out.

ForStaffLine manager

Opening the app

  • •On the branch phone or tablet: open the Jarz POS app icon.
  • •On a computer: open erp.orderjarz.com/pos/ in Chrome.
  • •Installing the app on a new phone: open erp.orderjarz.com/pos/download/ and install from there. Ask your manager before installing on a personal phone.

Signing in

  1. 1

    Type your username

    The Username field takes the account your manager created for you. It is usually your work email.

  2. 2

    Type your password

    Tap the eye icon to check what you typed before you submit.

  3. 3

    Tap Login

    Staff land on the Sales Kanban; line managers land on Point of Sale. Both can move between the two from the menu.

ℹYou stay signed in

You do not sign in again every morning. The app remembers you until you sign out, or until you close a shift - closing a shift signs you out on purpose, so the next person starts on their own account.

Switching the app to Arabic

  1. 1

    Open the menu

    Tap the ☰ icon in the header of POS or the order board. The menu is grouped: POS / Sales, Delivery / Logistics, Finance / Expenses, Purchasing / Inventory, and so on.

  2. 2

    Scroll to the bottom

    Below every menu group there is a Language switch showing the language you are on now.

  3. 3

    Flip it and confirm

    The app asks "Switch language to Arabic?". Confirm and the whole app flips, right to left included. Nothing about your orders changes.

Turning on order alerts

When a new online order arrives, the app rings an alarm and shows the order on top of whatever you are doing, with an Accept Order button. Accepting it moves the order onto the board and stops the alarm.

  • •The settings live on the Profile screen: open the order board and tap ⋮ (More Actions) in the header → Profile. It is not in the side menu. There you pick the alarm sound and see the roles you hold.
  • •On an iPhone using the web app: add the page to the Home Screen first, open it from there, then tap Enable Notifications on that Profile screen. Alerts will not arrive in a normal Safari tab.
  • •Only a line manager or above can mute the alarm. Staff can lower the handset volume but the mute switch is not theirs.

Signing out

Menu → Logout. Do this before handing the device to someone else - every order, shift and settlement is recorded against whoever is signed in.

When it will not let you in

▸"Invalid credentials"
The username or password is wrong. Check the keyboard language and that Caps Lock is off. Nobody at the branch can reset a password - ask your manager.
▸"Cannot reach server. Check Wi-Fi/VPN and backend URL, then try again."
This is the network, not your account. Check Wi-Fi, try mobile data, and try again. If other branches are working too, report it - do not keep retrying for an hour.
▸"No POS Profiles Available"
Your account is not attached to any branch yet. Your manager has to add you to the branch's POS profile before you can sell anything.
